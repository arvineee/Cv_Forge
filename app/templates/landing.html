The difference

Same experience, written to be read

Many CVs and resumes undersell good work. CVForge keeps your facts and rewrites them with the scope, numbers and results a recruiter looks for. Where a number is missing, it leaves a placeholder for you to fill in rather than inventing one.

Try it: one bullet, two versions

Alex M. Morgan

Sales Supervisor · Manchester

Experience

• Was in charge of sales at the shop and helped the team do better. Handled customers and stock.

• Led a team of 6 across 3 branches, growing monthly sales 32% in 8 months while cutting stock-outs by a quarter through weekly reorder checks.
