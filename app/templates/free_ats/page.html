What the ATS checker looks at

  • Contact details

    Email, phone number and a LinkedIn or portfolio link that tools and recruiters can find.

  • Section headings

    Standard headings such as Summary, Work Experience, Education and Skills.

  • Length

    Whether your CV fits a sensible one-to-two page range.

  • Numbers and results

    Real figures that show what your work achieved.

  • Writing style

    Bullet points, action verbs and direct wording instead of clichés.

  • Dates and keywords

    Clear, consistent dates, plus a keyword match against a job post if you add one.
