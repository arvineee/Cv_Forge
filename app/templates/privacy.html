Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

CVForge AI ("we", "us") is a CV/resume-building service operated in Kenya. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use CVForge AI, why, and what rights you have over it — including your rights under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), and if you sign in with Google, your Google account ID and profile info.
  • CV content: anything you enter or upload into the builder — work history, education, skills, contact details, and any CV/resume file you upload for parsing.
  • Payment data: your phone number (for M-Pesa) or card details (for card payments) are collected and processed by our payment processor, IntaSend — we do not see or store your full card number ourselves. We do store payment amounts, references, and status.
  • Usage data: pages you visit, timestamps, browser type, and a truncated IP address (last segment masked) — used to understand site traffic and diagnose problems. You can opt out of this being linked to your account in your account settings.
  • Communications: emails we send you (verification, password reset, payment confirmations, and occasional product updates you can opt out of).

3. How we use it

  • To provide the service — building, storing, and exporting your CV and cover letters
  • To process AI features (CV parsing, content improvement, ATS scoring, cover letter generation, and our support chat) — see Section 4 on third parties
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To send essential account emails (verification, password reset, payment receipts)
  • To send occasional, opt-out-able emails about features or plan upgrades — never more than a few times, and never if you've turned this off
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and enforce fair-use limits on our free plan

4. Third parties we share data with

We don't sell your data. We do share limited data with the following service providers, strictly to operate CVForge AI:

  • Google (Gemini API): when you use an AI feature (revamp, ATS check, cover letter, support chat), the relevant CV text or question is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. Google's own privacy terms govern how they process that request.
  • Google (Sign in with Google): if you use Google login, Google shares your name, email, and profile photo with us to create your account.
  • IntaSend: our payment processor. Your phone number or card details are sent directly to IntaSend to process M-Pesa or card payments — we don't handle raw card numbers ourselves.
  • Email delivery provider: used to send account and transactional emails.

We do not use your CV content to train any AI model, ours or a third party's.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and CV data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your CV content and personal data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to keep payment records for accounting/legal purposes. Site-traffic data is automatically deleted after 90 days.

6. Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data (most of this you can edit directly in your account)
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing (e.g. opt out of marketing emails or usage tracking, both available in account settings)
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these, email us at the address in Section 8.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed, not stored in plain text. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). We limit access to personal data to what's needed to operate the service. No system is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect your data.

8. Children's privacy

CVForge AI is not directed at children under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. We'll update the date at the top of this page when we do.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data — email privacy@cvforge.app, or use our support page.
