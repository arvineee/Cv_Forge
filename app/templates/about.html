Our Mission

CVForge AI was built with a simple conviction: good people lose opportunities not because of their skills, but because their CV or resume is hard to read, badly organised, or filtered out by applicant tracking software before a person ever sees it.

CVForge is built in Kenya, and we build for job seekers wherever they are, from graduates writing a first CV to experienced professionals changing country or career. CVForge helps you create a professional CV or resume, improve an existing one, match it to a job description and write a tailored cover letter.

No design skills needed. Choose a template, add your details, and download a clean PDF or Word file. You can pay by debit or credit card through Paystack, or by M-Pesa if you are in Kenya.
